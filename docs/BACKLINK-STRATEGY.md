# Authority and Backlink Strategy

- Build permissioned project pages that clients can link to naturally.
- Seek accurate listings from relevant Assam business associations, startup communities, and reputable local directories after verifying NAP details.
- Contribute useful, original material to local business, design, technology, or event publications when there is editorial fit.
- Maintain relationships with clients and collaborators; request a contextual credit only where it is useful to their audience.
- Track referring domain, target URL, anchor context, source quality, status, and referral/enquiry value.

Do not buy links, use PBNs, automate submissions, fabricate directory profiles, or manufacture reviews.
