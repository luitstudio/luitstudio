# Assam Local SEO Plan

## Implemented

The homepage, service introduction, contact details, metadata, and Organization schema consistently identify Luit Studio with Guwahati, Assam. The schema uses `areaServed` for Guwahati, Assam, Northeast India, and India without claiming an office or street address.

## Location-page decision

**NOT IMPLEMENTED:** `/locations/*` pages. The repository provides no verified clients, projects, offices, or location-specific material for Jorhat, Dibrugarh, Silchar, Tezpur, Nagaon, Tinsukia, Sivasagar, Bongaigaon, or North Lakhimpur. Creating them would risk doorway content.

Create a location page only after documenting genuine service availability plus unique local needs, a permissioned example or local resource, useful FAQs, and a substantive contact path.

## Manual local checklist

- **REQUIRES BUSINESS INFORMATION:** verify the exact legal/profile business name, primary category, service area, hours, phone, and public address eligibility.
- **REQUIRES MANUAL ACTION:** make website, email, phone, and service area match the verified Google Business Profile.
- **REQUIRES MANUAL ACTION:** add real work photos, respond to genuine reviews, and ask clients for reviews after completed work. Never use a residential address unless it is publicly eligible.
